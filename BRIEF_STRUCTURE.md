# Research Brief — template structure and the one change to make

Source: `research-brief-template.docx` (Udacity, Lesson Resources tab). Section names below are the template's own.
Fill it in Word and keep these headings: the rubric is graded against them.

**Header block:** Student Name · Date · Topic/Company

| § | Template section | Fields it asks for |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Research Objective | The business question or decision this supports |
| 2 | Scope of Research | Markets or industries covered · Competitor types included · Timeframe considered · Key constraints (e.g. limited research hours) |
| 3 | Research Approach | Types of questions asked · How research effort was prioritised · Tools used within Amazon Quick Suite |
| 4 | Key Market Insights | 3–5 insight blocks, each: Insight Summary · Supporting Evidence or Signals · Why This Insight Matters |
| 5 | Visual Evidence | Chart Type · What the visualization shows · How it supports the insight |
| 6 | Confidence Assessment | Source quality · Consistency across sources · Timeliness of information · Overall confidence level (High/Medium/Low) |
| 7 | Limitations and Risks | Missing data · Conflicting signals · Potential bias or ambiguity |
| 8 | Strategic Implications | What decisions could this inform? · **What should not be concluded from this research?** |
| 9 | Summary for Leadership | Executive summary, 5–7 sentences |

## The one place the template and the rubric disagree

The template prints **section 6 once**. The rubric says:

> "Research Brief includes a **Confidence Assessment for each insight** covering: source quality, consistency across
> sources, timeliness of information, and overall confidence level (High/Medium/Low)."

**So repeat section 6 per insight** — a short four-line block under each one, or a table in section 6 with one row per
insight and a column each for source quality, consistency, timeliness and overall level. A single overall assessment is
the kind of near-miss that cost us the NovaTech resubmission.

## Other rubric details the template does not spell out

- Section 5 is marked "Optional but Recommended" in the template, but the rubric requires **either** a referenced visual
  (with chart type, what it shows, how it supports an insight, plus a screenshot) **or** an explicit statement that no
  visuals were used. We are including a chart, so the screenshot is required.
- Section 7 needs **at least two** concrete items.
- Section 8 needs **at least two** explicit non-conclusions.
- Section 9 must be **5–7 sentences** — count them.
- Supporting evidence must be **specific and verifiable**: figures, named sources, referenced outputs.
- Screenshots showing AI-assisted work are required by the rubric, not optional.

## Writing rules carried over from the NovaTech failure

Define every term, acronym and product name where it first appears. No code, SQL words or formulas. Complete sentences
everywhere, including bullets and table cells.
